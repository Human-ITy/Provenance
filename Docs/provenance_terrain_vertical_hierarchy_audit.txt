========== WHERE THE ~10 m COMPRESSION OCCURS ==========
1. Feature amplitudes are GRADE units: peak prom~0.28-0.60, ridge crest~0.19-0.35,
   massif lift~0.22-0.26, smooth~0.45-1.01. Designed as tectonic.grade_at, not metres.
2. GradeToZ is the SINGLE scale: Z=(grade-0.5)*64*0.125 = *8 m/grade.
   Peak 0.60 * 8 = 4.8 m. Carrier contrast 0.56 * 8 = 4.5 m. Together ~10 m.
3. Massif lift is computed in SharpFromContext (ecology) but NOT added to SampleZ.
   Even if added, 0.26*8 = 2.1 m — still compressed by the same knob.
4. kGradeMax=2.60 clamp is NOT the live cause (raw at dominant peak stays < 2.3).
5. Valley incision term in SampleZ is hard-coded 0; drainage lives in 500 m smooth.
DO NOT globally multiply the reconstructed grade field: that would scale local
carrier and features together and turn lowlands into mountains.
