## EI3.V player-view continuity

EI3.V is certified for the canonical playable path. Its deterministic traversal
runs four exact 256 m legs at 30 m/s (north, return south, east, return west),
then descends and walks 48 m without pausing for terrain. The return checkpoints
must resolve to the same engine-issued `MacroFeatureId` at the same absolute
coordinate. This is an ancestry/continuity landmark gate; the origin is not
mislabelled as a `MacroPeakId`.

The certificate fails if any of the following occurs:

- a lower-frame sky hole appears beneath the expected terrain silhouette;
- the macro carrier becomes empty or changes source revision;
- detailed matter is absent at an ahead/endpoint checkpoint or during walking;
- landing/grounding differs from the published matter surface being rendered;
- the player becomes ungrounded while walking;
- a returned landmark coordinate changes its engine-issued ancestry;
- the diagnostic lattice or a local physics fixture enters normal play.

Coarse handoff frames while detail is still being prepared are counted but are
not failures. The coarse carrier remains visible until a complete matter-backed
replacement is published. Obsolete speculative requests may be cancelled, but
published terrain is never withdrawn to expose sky.

The certified receipt is `Docs/provenance_ei3v_traversal_cert.txt`; the five PNG
frames are endpoint/final visual evidence. EI3.Q remains closed. The current
8 m reconstruction can look washed or coarse; rocks, vegetation, micro-surface
breakup, richer strata presentation, and mountain-shape quality are explicitly
outside EI3.V.
