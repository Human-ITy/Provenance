# Causal Geography Floor

Status: proposed pre-Stage-12 macro proof. No implementation authority is opened
by this document.

## Purpose

Scale the certified Stage 5-11 geological history into a larger bare-earth
landform fixture. The proof succeeds only when the terrain reads as coherent
geography without vegetation, water presentation, atmosphere, props, or texture
detail carrying the result.

```text
canonical geological bodies and structure
  -> regional uplift / deformation
  -> material-dependent compiled erosion
  -> coherent ridges, valleys, scarps, saddles, basins, and shoulders
  -> present exposure re-queries the actual geology
```

Geology must be legible in topography before it is legible in material color.

## First fixture

Use one deterministic, bare region hundreds of metres across containing:

- a folded sandstone/shale belt;
- the certified granite intrusion;
- the certified contact-mineralized quartz body;
- the certified fault;
- one bounded regional uplift/erosion history.

The result must contain a coherent sandstone ridge, shale-recessed valley,
granite shoulder or massif, fault-offset landform, and drainage low. These are
causal landforms, not named noise masks or material paint.

## Entrance gates

- Stage 0 and Stages 5-11 retain their authority, geometry, material/FeatureId,
  collision, and transition-order certificates.
- The cardinal streaming and visual return gate passes on +X, -X, +Y, and -Y.
- Ordinary local traversal remains below the 16.667 ms frame budget on the test
  machine; hostile bulk-travel cost is reported separately.
- No sky tears, fallback colors, live/far seams, collision disagreement, or
  return pop is accepted.

## Causal gates

- Equalizing sandstone/shale resistance removes their systematic relief
  separation.
- Removing regional uplift collapses the associated mountain/ridge system.
- Removing the fault removes the offset landform without changing older bodies.
- Removing the intrusion removes granite landforms and contact mineralization,
  not unrelated terrain.
- Moving the present surface causes exposure to be re-queried; the compiler may
  not repaint materials to fit a preferred shape.
- Monolithic and tiled compilation produce the same geometric and geological
  answers.
- Near, live, handoff, and far representations share the same surface boundary
  within their declared tolerances.

## Explicitly closed

No Stage-12 breach implementation, water coupling, active erosion, sediment
transport, soils, biomes, flora, bodies, mutation, or P5b is opened here.

## Exit sentence

The undressed terrain is already a believable place whose mountains, ridges,
valleys, scarps, and basins are consequences of the certified geological
history rather than generic noise or material paint.
